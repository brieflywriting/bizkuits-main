Meet Biz'Kuitz! Recently expelled dismissed from his position as a Court Wizard, this Khajiit scholar is searching for a more adventurous life. He can be found in the bookshop Tail Tales in Old Ebonheart. Although not skilled in Combat, Biz'Kuitz can be quite a handy companion for your travels. He can cast all sorts of Beneficial Spells for you, share his delicious Moon Sugar Biscuits and even pay you to keep him safe while accompanying him on his travels across the Dunmer Province of Morrowind.


REQUIRES: Tamriel Rebuilt
OPTIONALS: Sun's Dusk and S3maphore
